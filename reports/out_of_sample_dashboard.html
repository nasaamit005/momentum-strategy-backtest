Methodology and Limitations

These results use the supplied out-of-sample summary CSV. The evaluation window begins at the holdout date in that file. Earlier prices can be used to warm up moving-average and momentum indicators, but returns before the holdout date should not be counted in holdout performance.

Historical backtest performance does not guarantee future results. Validate signal timing, missing-price handling, execution assumptions, corporate-action treatment, transaction costs, and out-of-sample methodology before making investment or resume claims about performance.
